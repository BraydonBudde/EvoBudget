Frequently asked questions

What is Ezzo Hub?
Ezzo Hub is a growing collection of straightforward, no-nonsense tools for everyday life admin: Ezzo Budget, task planning, habit tracking, wedding planning, meal planning, and fitness planning - all sharing the same simple design and the same respect for your data.
What tools does Ezzo Hub include?
Ezzo Budget (with two planners to choose from: Simple and Ultimate) is live today. A Task Planner, Habit Tracker, Wedding Planner, Meal Planner, and Fitness Planner are all coming soon. Each one follows the same principles: fast to start, free to try, and built around keeping your data under your control.
Do I need an account to use Ezzo Hub?
No. Ezzo Budget is available today and you can start using it immediately - no account needed. If a tool offers optional cloud sync, that's always opt-in - never required to get started.
Is Ezzo Hub free?
Ezzo Budget is free to try today. Where a paid upgrade exists, it's a single one-time purchase rather than a subscription - so you pay once and keep using it, including future updates. Every future tool will follow the same model.
How is my data stored?
By default, your data is stored locally on your own device - nothing is sent to a server. Where a tool offers optional sync, your data goes to your own personal cloud storage, never to our servers.
When will the other tools launch?
We're building them one at a time so each one is solid, not rushing all five out at once. Hit "Notify me" on any tool's card in the section above and we'll reach out the moment it's ready.
