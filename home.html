Frequently asked questions

What is Evo Hub?
Evo Hub is a growing collection of straightforward, no-nonsense tools for everyday life admin. It starts with Evo Budget, a personal budget planner, and will keep growing with tools like task planning, habit tracking, and wedding planning - all sharing the same simple design and the same respect for your data.
What tools are available right now?
Evo Budget is live today, with two planners to choose from: the Simple Budget Planner and the Ultimate Budget Planner. Both are free to try, with no bank login and no subscription required.
What's coming next?
We're working on a Task Planner, Habit Tracker, Wedding Planner, Meal Planner, and Fitness Planner. Each one will follow the same principles as Evo Budget: fast to start, free to try, and built around keeping your data under your control.
Do I need an account to use Evo Hub?
No. You can open any live tool and start using it immediately. If a tool offers optional cloud sync, that's always opt-in - never required to get started.
Is Evo Hub free?
Every tool offers a free way to try it. Where a paid upgrade exists, it's a single one-time purchase rather than a subscription - so you pay once and keep using it, including future updates to that tool.
How is my data stored?
By default, your data is stored locally on your own device - nothing is sent to a server. Where a tool offers optional sync, your data goes to your own personal cloud storage, never to our servers.
