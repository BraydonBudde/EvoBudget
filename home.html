Frequently asked questions

What is Evo Hub?
Evo Hub is a collection of straightforward, no-nonsense tools for everyday life admin: Evo Budget, task planning, habit tracking, wedding planning, meal planning, and fitness planning - all sharing the same simple design and the same respect for your data.
What tools does Evo Hub include?
Evo Budget (with two planners to choose from: Simple and Ultimate), a Task Planner, a Habit Tracker, a Wedding Planner, a Meal Planner, and a Fitness Planner. Each one follows the same principles: fast to start, free to try, and built around keeping your data under your control.
Do I need an account to use Evo Hub?
No. You can open any tool and start using it immediately. If a tool offers optional cloud sync, that's always opt-in - never required to get started.
Is Evo Hub free?
Every tool offers a free way to try it. Where a paid upgrade exists, it's a single one-time purchase rather than a subscription - so you pay once and keep using it, including future updates to that tool.
How is my data stored?
By default, your data is stored locally on your own device - nothing is sent to a server. Where a tool offers optional sync, your data goes to your own personal cloud storage, never to our servers.
