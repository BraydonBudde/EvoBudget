Credit. A sale is yours when the buyer uses your discount code, or buys within the link window after clicking your link. A code always wins over a link. Purchases you make yourself, through your own link or code, earn no commission.

Refunds. If an order is refunded, its commission is cancelled. If it was already paid to you, the amount is taken off your next payout.

Honest promotion. Always label posts as an ad or affiliate link where your platform or law requires it (for example #ad). Don't bid on "Ezzo Hub" or "Ezzo" in search ads, don't post coupon codes on coupon sites, don't invent reviews or results, and don't make claims about the product it doesn't make. No spam.

Your data, theirs. You see clicks, demo starts, sales and earnings. You never see a buyer's name, email or budget, and they never see yours.

Changes and ending. We may update these terms or the rates with notice by email; earned commission is always honoured at the rate it was earned. Either side can end the partnership any time. Accounts that break these terms can be closed and unpaid commission withheld.

Taxes. You're responsible for any tax on what you earn.
