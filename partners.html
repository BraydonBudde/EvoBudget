Credit. A sale is yours when the buyer uses your discount code, or buys within the link window after clicking your link. A code always wins over a link. Purchases you make yourself, through your own link or code, earn no commission.

Honest promotion. Always label posts as an ad or affiliate link where your platform or law requires it (for example #ad). Your data, theirs. You see clicks, demo starts, sales and earnings. You never see a buyer's name, email or budget, and they never see yours.
