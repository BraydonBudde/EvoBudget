Enter every debt and pick a strategy: Snowball for the smallest balance first, or Avalanche for the highest rate first. Add an extra monthly payment and watch the debt free date move.

It handles credit cards (percent of balance minimums with a floor), mortgages (escrow, fixed or adjustable rates, equal or declining payments), student loans, car loans and personal loans, and it can auto calculate the minimum payment from the loan term. Open the full month by month payment schedule for any debt.

💳 SUBSCRIPTION TRACKER
Every recurring charge with its billing date and cost, so the small ones stop being invisible.

🎯 SINKING FUNDS
For the big one that arrives twice a year. Set the target and the date you need it by, and each fund tells you exactly how much to put aside per month to get there on time. Log a contribution with one tap, and a Goal reached badge appears when you make it.

🗓️ SMART CALENDAR
Paydays, bills, debt payments and subscriptions on one month view. Click any day to see what happens on it.

🤖 EZZO, YOUR AI BUDGET ASSISTANT
Ask questions about your own budget in plain language. Ezzo reads your numbers and answers. It can never change them. Bring your own free Google Gemini key: it is encrypted on your device and never leaves it.

⚙️ AUTOMATION
Set a bill, a debt minimum or a subscription to log itself on schedule, so the routine ones stop needing you.

📈 CHARTS THAT SAY SOMETHING
Cash flow expected against actual, where your income comes from, where your spending goes, and a daily spend line across the period.

🔄 ROLLOVER
Carry what you did not spend into the next period instead of losing track of it.

📅 WORKS WITH YOUR PAY SCHEDULE
Set your budget period to any date range: monthly, biweekly, weekly, or whatever odd shape matches how you really get paid. This month and Last month are one click.
