Questions

Good to know.

What is the Ultimate Budget Planner?
A budget planner that runs in your browser and shows you one clear number: what you can spend until payday, with your bills already set aside. It also tracks your spending, bills and subscriptions, savings goals and debts, and includes a calendar, savings challenges and Ezzo, a budget assistant you can ask questions in plain words.
Can I try it for free?
Yes. The free demo has every feature, with room for 10 transactions, 3 bills and subscriptions, 3 savings goals, 3 debts and 5 categories in each part of your budget. You don't need an account or a card, and there is nothing to install.
Is it a subscription?
No. It is a single payment of $49.99, and updates are included. There are no monthly or yearly fees.
Do I need to connect my bank account?
No. You never share your bank login. You log what you spend in a few taps, or import a statement from your bank as a CSV or PDF file.
Where is my data stored?
On your own device, in your browser. In the full planner you can turn on Google sync, which backs your budget up to your own Google Drive and keeps it the same on all your devices. Your budget is never stored on our servers.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. It works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and nothing needs installing. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
How do I unlock it after I buy?
You receive a license key after your purchase. Open this page, choose "I have a key", paste it in, and the full planner opens. Everything you set up in the demo stays where it is.
What if I'm paid weekly, or my pay changes?
Your budget can run weekly, every two or four weeks, monthly from your payday, by calendar month, or on any dates you choose. If your pay varies, you just log it when it lands.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. You can export all your transactions to a CSV file at any time and open it in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
Which languages does it support?
The planner is in English. Bank statements in several other languages can still be imported.
