What is the Ultimate Budget Planner?
A budget planner that shows what's safe to spend until payday, with your bills already set aside. It also covers bills, goals, debt, a calendar, savings challenges and Ezzo, your budget assistant.
How does the daily amount work?
It takes your pay, sets your bills aside first, then spreads what's left across the days until payday. Spend less today and tomorrow's amount grows.
Can I try it for free?
Yes. The free demo has every feature, so you can set it up with your own numbers and see your real daily amount. No card or account needed.
Is it a subscription?
It's one payment of $49.99, not a subscription. Updates are included.
Do I need to connect my bank?
No. You log spending yourself, or import a bank statement as a CSV or PDF.
Where is my data stored?
On your device. In the full planner you can also sync it to your own Google Drive. It's never stored on our servers.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes, in any modern browser on any device. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
How do I unlock it after buying?
You'll get a license key. Tap "I have a key", paste it in, and the full planner opens. Your demo setup stays where it is.
What if I'm paid weekly, or my pay changes?
It works weekly, every two or four weeks, monthly, or on your own dates. If your pay varies, just log it when it lands.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. Export your transactions to a CSV file any time.
Which languages does it support?
The planner is in English. It can still read bank statements in several other languages.
Who is Ezzo?
Ezzo is the assistant built into the planner. Ask in plain words, like "Can I afford a $35 dinner?", and get a straight answer.
